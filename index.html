Privacy Policy

SnapClip Browser Extension — Last updated: May 27, 2026

TL;DR: SnapClip works 100% locally on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared with anyone. Your screenshots never leave your browser.

1. Data Collection

SnapClip does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No cookies or fingerprinting
  • No user accounts or registration
  • No external network requests
  • No telemetry of any kind

2. Screenshot Storage

All screenshots are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. They are never uploaded to any server. You can delete all stored screenshots at any time through the History page.

Settings are stored locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API, which syncs preferences across your signed-in Chrome browsers. No third party can access this data.

3. Permissions Explained

SnapClip requests the following browser permissions, each for a specific technical reason:

  • activeTab — Capture the currently visible tab when you click a capture button
  • scripting — Inject the selection overlay and scroll-stitch logic for full-page captures
  • tabs — Read the page title and URL to label your screenshots in history
  • clipboardWrite — Copy screenshots directly to your clipboard
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — Save screenshots locally in IndexedDB and sync settings
  • offscreen — Write to clipboard from the background when the popup is closed
  • notifications — Show a notification when capturing on restricted browser pages
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — Required for full-page scroll capture and Site Capture on any website. Without this, the extension cannot capture pages opened programmatically.

4. Site Capture

The Site Capture feature discovers same-origin links on the current page and opens them one at a time to take full-page screenshots. All link discovery and capture happens locally in your browser. No data about discovered URLs or captured pages is sent anywhere.

5. Third-Party Libraries

SnapClip bundles JSZip (MIT license) for ZIP file generation. JSZip runs entirely in-browser and makes no network requests.

6. Children's Privacy

SnapClip does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be published with the next extension update. The "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
